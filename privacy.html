Privacy Policy — Himalaya Email

This policy describes a personal Gmail integration operated by the owner of the GitHub account rus5star. Contact: rus5@outlook.com.

Google data accessed and its purpose

With the operator’s consent, the integration accesses email messages, message metadata, attachments, and mailbox labels as needed to carry out requested email tasks. Permissions may include reading, composing, sending, organizing, and deleting messages. The integration is intended for the operator’s own account, not for collecting other people’s Google account credentials.

Processing and sharing

Himalaya communicates with Google’s Gmail services. Hermes can process email information to fulfill the operator’s requests. Depending on the model and services selected in Hermes, email information included in an assistant conversation may be sent to the selected external AI provider or processed by a local model. Information can also be included in conversation history or configured memory services. This integration should not be interpreted as guaranteeing that all email processing stays on the operator’s computer.

Email contents are not published on this website. The operator does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising. Sending or forwarding messages, or exporting information to another service, is performed at the operator’s direction.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s computer and used to authenticate with Google. Retrieved information may be retained in local files, assistant history, configured memory storage, or by a selected external provider according to that provider’s policies. Credentials and mailbox data are not intended to be committed to this public website repository.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls local records and configured services. Revoking Google authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing email copies, conversation history, or memory records. Those records must be removed separately through the relevant application or service.

Control and revocation

The operator can revoke this application’s access through Google Account connections. Questions or deletion requests can be directed to the contact address above.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used for the operator’s requested email features, not to develop or train generalized AI models.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the integration’s data practices change.
