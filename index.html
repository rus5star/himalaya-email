Himalaya Email

Himalaya Email is a personal email integration operated by the owner of the GitHub account rus5star. It connects the Himalaya email client and Hermes assistant to the operator’s own Gmail account using Google OAuth authorization.

Purpose

The integration can list, search, read, organize, and send email as directed by its operator, within the permissions granted through Google. It is for personal use, not a public email service or an official Google product.

Authorization

Google handles sign-in and consent. Authorization credentials are stored on the operator’s computer and transmitted to Google when needed to authenticate requests. This public website contains only application information, not mailbox contents or credentials.
